A smaller, tidier build; nothing changes in how the app works.

The app is now shrunk at build time, which takes the download from about 8.7 MB to about 1.1 MB.

It also no longer asks for the network-state permission. That came in with a background-task library the app uses, which declares it for jobs that wait for a connection; none of the app's jobs do, and the app never goes online.
